0.1 UNIQUE EDITORIAL THESIS
============================================================

Before writing, define one specific, defensible thesis for the article. A topic is not a thesis.

Weak topic:
“Website costs in Switzerland.”

Stronger thesis:
“The least expensive Swiss website quotation can become the most expensive option once ownership, maintenance, missing conversion work and future migration are calculated.”

Weak topic:
“AI phone assistants for hotels.”

Stronger thesis:
“Hotel reception teams often lose bookings not at night, but during the ten minutes when the receptionist is helping the guest directly in front of them.”

The thesis must:
- guide the entire article;
- appear clearly near the beginning;
- be supported by evidence, examples or transparent reasoning;
- not be reusable in another article without changing its meaning;
- lead naturally to the matching Artifact.

Reject or redesign any article whose thesis could be copied into another planned article.
