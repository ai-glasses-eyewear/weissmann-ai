AI Receptionist KPI Dashboard

“Calls answered” sits near 100% for any working system by design — which is exactly why it says almost nothing. Enter your own call-log numbers and this dashboard computes the twelve figures that actually matter, labels the vanity ones, and flags the combinations that hide a problem.

No benchmark to chase. There is no credible published Swiss or international benchmark for any of these figures. The only comparison worth making is your own number against your own number from an earlier period — a rolling four-week window, held steady only once a change survives three or four windows. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is stored. Please enter counts only, no caller names or personal data.

Cross-checks — where a number hides a problem

If you track only one pair this month, make it Resolution Rate and Repeat-Call Rate, read together over a rolling four weeks — between them they come closest to answering the one question that matters: not whether the phone got picked up, but whether the caller got what they called for.

Note: a decision aid, not a scoreboard. Bad-Answer Rate is the one figure no system self-reports — it needs a person to sample and read transcripts on a fixed schedule. Average Handling Time is a number to watch, not a target to push. Below a few dozen calls a period, read the transcripts instead of the percentages.
