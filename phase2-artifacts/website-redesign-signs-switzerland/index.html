Website Redesign Triage

Not every dated website needs a rebuild — but some problems quietly cost you customers every week, while others are just annoying. Tick the warning signs you recognise on your own site. The twelve are grouped by what they cost, not by how they look, and the verdict updates as you go.

The honest framing: this is a triage, not a sales funnel. Some sites here will land on “targeted fixes, not a rebuild”, and a few on “nothing urgent — leave it be.” A redesign is only overdue when the revenue-blocking signs stack up.
