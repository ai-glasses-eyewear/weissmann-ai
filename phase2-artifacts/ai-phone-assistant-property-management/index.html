Tenant-Call Triage Rule Builder

An AI phone assistant can safely log a dripping tap, book a viewing, or send a rent statement. What it must never do is decide how dangerous a call is. Assemble a triage rule-set that keeps those two jobs — routine administration and emergency judgement — strictly separate, then copy the sheet.

The governing principle: the assistant can own the administrative job completely — listen, categorise, route, log. It should never be the one deciding how dangerous a situation is. Blending the two is exactly where automated systems quietly fail tenants.
