Which AI-receptionist buyer are you?

There is no single best AI receptionist in Switzerland — the guide compares six Swiss providers by buyer type instead of forcing them into one ranked list. Answer five quick questions and this tool matches you to one of its four buyer personas, then shows what to prioritise, what to ask every vendor, and what to watch out for.

Disclosure: Weissmann publishes this guide and is one of the six providers compared (alongside AlpenAgent, Suisse Voice, NEX-AI, HeyPapaya and Alveni). It is not the fit for every persona — for a hotelier with existing property software the guide names a different provider plainly. Every fact below reflects each provider's own public site, accessed 29 July 2026.
