Speed to first results

Ads: most campaigns clear Google's ad review within a business day. SEO: Google's own guidance says some changes take days, others months — wait several weeks before judging.

Cost over time

Ads: priced per auction, roughly flat per visitor while it runs — Weissmann's Ads Growth is CHF 690/month management, with ad spend paid separately to Google. SEO: Weissmann's SEO Growth is CHF 890/month (audit, on-page, monitoring; content writing, development and backlink outreach not included), front-loaded then flatter.

Durability

A paused Ads account is a light switch — visibility stops the same day. A neglected SEO position is a slow leak — it fades, it doesn't switch off. Independent research (Backlinko) puts the #1 organic result's average click-through rate around 39.8%.

Reading it for your own business

  • A deadline already exists (an opening, a launch, a season) and the result must be visible before it arrives → Ads.
  • No deadline, but a keyword worth owning for years, and you can commit content for 2–3 months before judging → SEO.
  • The budget is tight and every franc must be defensible this month → the flatter, front-loaded SEO fee, or a small, tightly-capped Ads test.
  • The product isn't provable yet (no landing page worth sending traffic to, no way to track a lead) → neither yet — fix the site and tracking first.
