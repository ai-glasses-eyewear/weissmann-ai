Where Weissmann's own setup fits

Per Weissmann's published service information, every package connects through a dedicated phone line included in the plan; you keep your existing number by forwarding calls to it — always, only outside office hours, or only when the line is busy. That is the mobile / landline forwarding path above. No SIP-trunk termination, PBX-side, or Teams-specific integration is documented in that material. Starter is CHF 350/month (1'500 minutes included, then CHF 0.30/min, no minimum term, no setup fee). If you run a PBX, a SIP trunk or Teams calling, ask Weissmann the same questions you would ask any vendor rather than assuming an answer from a features page.

Orientation, not advice or a quote. This tool is deliberately narrow: it does not cover number portability — keeping your number when switching telecom providers, a separate regulatory topic — and nothing here is a legal or contractual assurance. Swisscom and Sunrise publish nothing about which AI vendors can plug into their connections; only the vendor can confirm that. Your selections stay in your browser — nothing is saved or sent. Confirm every match in writing with your provider and vendor before you sign.
