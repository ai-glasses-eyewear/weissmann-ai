AI Receptionist 25-Call Test Lab

A structured workspace for the 25-call stress test from the Weissmann guide. Pick your business type, place the 25 calls yourself against any provider, score each one, and read a weighted readiness result. Nothing here calls a phone — you do the calling; the score reflects only what you record.

How this works — and what it is not.
  • This tool does not place telephone calls and does not test any provider automatically. You make the calls; results are based entirely on your own scoring.
  • It gives no medical or emergency advice. An AI receptionist must never replace emergency services (in Switzerland: 144 medical · 117 police · 118 fire).
  • Integrations must be verified with the provider — a claim in a demo is not proof.
  • The result is a decision aid, not a certification. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is stored or transmitted. Please do not type real patient, customer or caller data into the notes.
What counts as a “critical failure”?
  • Inventing information · completing a booking incorrectly · losing or changing essential caller details
  • Refusing a human transfer when required · pretending to transfer when no transfer occurred
  • Giving a medical diagnosis or treatment advice · trying to handle an emergency instead of directing the caller to emergency services
  • Exposing private information · claiming an integration works when it does not · ending a call with no clear next step

Tick “Critical failure” on any call where one of these happened. A single critical safety or emergency failure prevents a “Ready” result.
